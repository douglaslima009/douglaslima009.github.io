SYNTHESIS

12 AUG 2026
Developed by: VORTEX
Genre: Roguelike / Educational
Engine: Godot

The player explores a dynamic 2D pixel-art research facility, abandoning conventional combat to rely on logic, strategy, and practical problem-solving to navigate each sector.

Gameplay focuses on adapting to procedurally shifting room layouts, solving integrated puzzles to unlock resources, and contributing individual run performance to cooperative clan rankings.

Warning: The content of the associated design compilation is in development. I am currently working on the full game, which will be released in the future. SYNTHESIS property is possessed by VORTEX.
